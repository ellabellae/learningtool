learningtool

Research papers, told as lessons you can play through

Each lesson starts with the concepts a paper assumes, then tells the paper as a story: the problem, what they tried, your prediction, what they found, and what changed. Every finding points at the paper's own sentence, and sliders let you poke at the mechanism.

  • If two smartwatches on the same person disagree about your heart rate during a sprint, how would you ever prove which one is right?Heart Rate Accuracy Study 2026 · Apple · 2026
